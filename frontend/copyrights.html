Meet Us...

We are Dharun Prasad and Jay Suriya, and this is our project, “Collegenator” — inspired by the classic Akinator experience, adapted for our college community (currently focused on UG-2 students).
The project was built by manually curating publicly available information from online sources to create an interactive and engaging guessing experience tailored to our campus.
All information used in this project is sourced from publicly accessible platforms and is intended purely for educational, experimental, and entertainment purposes.
We hope you enjoy trying it out — feel free to share it with others!
